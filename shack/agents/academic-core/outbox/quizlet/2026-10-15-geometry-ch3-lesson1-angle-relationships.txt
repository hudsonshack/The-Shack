Parallel lines	Coplanar lines that never intersect and have the same slope
Perpendicular lines	Lines that intersect at a 90 degree angle; slopes are negative reciprocals
Skew lines	Non-coplanar lines that are not parallel and never intersect
Parallel planes	Planes that do not intersect
Transversal	A line that crosses two or more coplanar lines at different points
Vertical angles	Opposite angles formed by two intersecting lines; always congruent
Linear pair	Two adjacent angles whose non-shared sides form a straight line; supplementary (sum 180)
Alternate interior angles	Angles between the two lines on opposite sides of the transversal; congruent if lines are parallel
Alternate exterior angles	Angles outside the two lines on opposite sides of the transversal; congruent if lines are parallel
Corresponding angles	Angles in the same position at each intersection of the transversal; congruent if lines are parallel
Same side interior angles	Angles between the two lines on the same side of the transversal; supplementary if lines are parallel
Same side exterior angles	Angles outside the two lines on the same side of the transversal; supplementary if lines are parallel
Supplementary angles	Two angles whose measures add to 180 degrees
Complementary angles	Two angles whose measures add to 90 degrees
Congruent angles	Angles with equal measures
Adjacent angles	Angles that share a vertex and a side and have no interior points in common
If two parallel lines are cut by a transversal, corresponding angles are...	Congruent
If two parallel lines are cut by a transversal, alternate interior angles are...	Congruent
If two parallel lines are cut by a transversal, same side interior angles are...	Supplementary
Converse of corresponding angles postulate	If corresponding angles are congruent, the lines are parallel
Two lines are cut by a transversal and one pair of alternate interior angles is 70 and 70. Are the lines parallel?	Yes, congruent alternate interior angles imply parallel lines
Angle 1 = 55 and is vertical to angle 3. Angle 3 = ?	55 degrees
Angle 1 = 55 forms a linear pair with angle 2. Angle 2 = ?	125 degrees
Same side interior angles are 4x and 5x+9 with parallel lines. Find x.	4x + 5x + 9 = 180, so x = 19
Slope of parallel lines	Equal slopes
Slope of perpendicular lines	Product of slopes is -1 (negative reciprocals)
Line with slope 2/3: slope of a perpendicular line?	-3/2
Are skew lines coplanar?	No
Can two parallel lines be skew?	No, parallel lines are coplanar
In a rectangular box, an edge and a non-touching edge that is not parallel to it are...	Skew
Vertical angles theorem	Vertical angles are congruent
Linear pair postulate	If two angles form a linear pair, they are supplementary
Angle relationships that are congruent for parallel lines	Alternate interior, alternate exterior, corresponding
Angle relationships that are supplementary for parallel lines	Same side interior, same side exterior, linear pair
